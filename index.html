The Problem

Framing layout shouldn't be harder than framing.

Measuring, marking, checking, and rechecking stud locations takes time. StudLine is being developed around one simple idea: make the layout itself work harder for the framer.

```
```
Why StudLine

Built around the job.

StudLine is being engineered with the realities of framing in mind — speed, precision, simplicity, and repeatability.

01 / SPEED

Less repetitive measuring.

Spend less time repeatedly measuring stud locations and more time actually building.

02 / PRECISION

Clearer layout.

StudLine is designed to make stud locations immediately visible after the line is snapped.

03 / WORKFLOW

Designed for framers.

Instead of changing the way people frame, StudLine is being developed around the tools and workflow framers already understand.

```
```
The Idea

One line.
Every stud.

StudLine takes a familiar tool — the chalkline — and approaches it from a different angle. The goal is simple: give the framer a clearer layout with less repetitive work.

```
```
Built, Tested, Improved

This isn't just an idea.

StudLine is being developed through research, CAD, prototyping, testing, analysis, and continuous iteration.

01 / ENGINEERING

Design the solution.

Digital models and physical prototypes allow us to test mechanisms, materials, dimensions, and usability before moving toward a finished product.

02 / TESTING

Challenge the idea.

We test prototypes to discover what works, what fails, and what needs to change. Each result becomes part of the next iteration.
