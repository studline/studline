The StudLine Team

Different strengths. One direction.

StudLine exists because research, engineering, manufacturing, and business have to work together. Each member of the team brings a different piece of the process.

01
Research • Analysis • Testing

Coby Walker

Coby drives the research, analysis, documentation, and testing behind StudLine. His role is to investigate the problem, evaluate ideas, record results, and help turn testing into better design decisions.

02
Business • Marketing • Strategy

Cash Stuart

Cash brings the entrepreneurial side of the team. He focuses on marketing, customer awareness, business strategy, and understanding how StudLine can become a product people actually want to use.

03
Engineering • CAD • Manufacturing

Elijah Weers

Elijah turns ideas into physical designs. He works in CAD, models components, manages 3D printers, lasers, and the steel mill, and leads the hands-on construction that turns concepts into prototypes.
