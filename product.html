The Product

One line.
Every stud.

StudLine is a purpose-built chalkline concept for framing. Instead of treating every stud location as a separate measurement, StudLine is designed to make the layout process faster and more intuitive.

PRODUCT PROTOTYPE
STUDLINE
Designed Around the Job

Simple idea. Serious engineering.

The goal isn't to add another complicated tool to the framer's belt. The goal is to make a familiar process better.

01 / LAYOUT

Make stud placement visible.

StudLine is designed around making the locations of studs immediately apparent after the line is snapped.

02 / PRECISION

Reduce repetitive measuring.

A layout tool should help the user spend less time repeating measurements and checking marks.

03 / WORKFLOW

Built around real framing.

The product is being developed with construction workflows and real-world usability in mind.

04 / ITERATION

Engineered, tested, improved.

StudLine is being continuously designed, prototyped, tested and refined by our team.

Built to Be Used
